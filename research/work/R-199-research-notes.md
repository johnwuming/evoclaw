## Tool comparison (solo dev)
- Notion: flexible, free solo, over-engineering risk, sluggish
- Linear: fast, keyboard-first, cycles (sprints), 250 issues free
- GitHub Projects: code-integrated, free, limited reporting
- Trello: visual kanban, free
- Todoist: simple, $3/mo
- ClickUp: comprehensive, $5/mo
